# Nulu Harness Brand Asset Usage Guidelines


Nulu Harness is developed and maintained by World App Technologies as an independent project derived from the MIT-licensed Nulu Harness codebase. "Nulu Harness", the Nulu mark, and the Nulu wordmark are brand assets of World App Technologies. The Nulu name and marks belong to their own owner and this project does not grant any right to use them.

To keep the Nulu Harness ecosystem healthy, avoid user confusion, and make related resources easy to find and identify, we have established these specifications and hope that everyone will adhere to them:

- In your project's descriptive text, you may use "Nulu Harness" to truthfully and accurately describe your project's relationship with Nulu Harness, for example, "built on Nulu Harness" or "compatible with Nulu Harness." Such descriptions comply with license requirements and help users understand your project's positioning.
- If you wish your project name to reflect its association with the Nulu Harness ecosystem, we recommend using the "Nulu" designation, which is clear and facilitates communication within the community.
- When naming your project, please avoid using the full "Nulu Harness" name directly. Unauthorized use in project names can easily lead to user misunderstanding and confusion, thereby affecting the clarity of the entire ecosystem. It may also involve trademark infringement.
- Additionally, please avoid using official brand materials in your promotions or presentations in a way that could cause misunderstanding, so as not to give users the false impression of official endorsement, cooperation, or authorization.

We believe that a clear and orderly community environment will make every developer's efforts more visible and more readily recognized. For the few cases that do not comply with the above specifications, we may contact the relevant parties to make appropriate adjustments in order to maintain the overall order of the ecosystem. Thank you for your understanding and support—let us work together to build a more friendly and sustainable open-source community.
